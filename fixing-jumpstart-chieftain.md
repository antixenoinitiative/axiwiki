# Prebuilt AX Chieftain

The AX Combat Jumpstart Chieftain from the ARX-Store tries to cover too many AX roles at once, making it perform poorly in any one role. (Part of its loadout is also obsolete).

The mixed loadout, sacrificed internal-space, and shielded setup do not make use of the Chieftain's strengths, or worse, even work actively against them. See [Ship Build Theory](<https://wiki.antixenoinitiative.com/en/shipbuildtheory>) for why these compromises matter.

## ⚠️ Verdict: Not recommended

The jumpstart prebuild is fundamentally flawed, and the effort required to fix it largely negates the intended shortcut for all but the newest Commanders. **There are better ways to spend your ARX.**

### What to do with the Prebuild if you already have it

Okay, you already purchased it, what now? 
The Chieftain is a great AX ship, the prebuild can be saved, but it will take some work. 

The engineered Thrusters are its best aspect. The cheaper rebuy may be useful for very new Commanders, even if it does little to offset the build's poor effectiveness and survivability, often leading to more deaths overall in its stock configuration. 
The included cosmetics might be interesting to Commanders looking for *exactly* that kit and paintjob...


# How to fix it

Follow the Guide below towards fixing the prebuild into a completed AX ship-build, ordered in steps by priority and effort involved.

## Step A: Easy Module changes

These take the least effort and cost. Go to any Station with good Outfitting stock. **No Unlocks required.**

After finishing Step A, your ship should look similar to [this](https://edsy.org/#/L=J900000H4C0S40,LYS10LYS10L3I10LXC10LXC10Eht10,Cjw00Cjw00Cjw00Cjw00,9on00AAA00APoG05I_W0Adt00Aty30B8g00BLe00Bb600,15O0015O0015O00,25S006kK20072000Iw2020m001-C00,AX_0Chieftan_0A).

1. Remove the 1E Research Limpet Controller & 1A Repair Limpet Controller.
		Replace them with 1D & 2D Module Reinforcement Packages.
2. Remove the 4D Module Reinforcement Package from the military-slot.
		Replace it with a 4D Hull Reinforcement Package.
3. Remove the 6C Shield Generator.
		Replace it with a 5D Module Reinforcement Package.
4. Remove the 5D Hull Reinforcement Package.
		Replace it with a 5D Repair Limpet Controller.
5. Replace the Frameshift Drive with a newer SCO version.
    
The Nanite torpedoes are obsolete, without any purpose.

- Remove the 2I Guardian Nanite Torpedo Pylon.
		Replace it with a fixed Remote-release Flak Launcher OR an extra fixed or gimballed Beam Laser.

## Step B: Internal upgrades & Guardian modules

The next step involves crucial fixes to the Weapon loadout and Internal upgrades. **Guardian Unlocks & Engineering will be required**.

[This](<https://edsy.org/#/L=J900000H4C0S40,LYS10LYS10L3I10LXC10LXC10EhtH06v_W0,CjwG-2G_W0CjwG-2G_W0CjwG-2G_W0CjwG-2G_W0,9on00AAAG02K_W0APoG05I_W0AdtG04J_W0Aty30B8gG03L_W0BLe00Bb600,15O0015O0015O00,25S006kK20072000Iw2020m001-C00,AX_0Chieftan_0B>) will be the build you are working towards in Step B. It will have the correct loadout, and minimum required engineering.

### Engineering
List of the minimum recommended engineering steps.
> See our detailed Guide on minimum Engineer Unlocks and optimal travel routes: [Unlocking AX Engineers](https://wiki.antixenoinitiative.com/en/Unlocking-Engineers).
{.is-info}

1. Engineer any beam lasers you have:
	Long-Range engineering (at least G3) **+ Thermal Vent Experimental**. Up to Grade 3 can be done in `WYRD by The Dweller`.
2. The Distributor is (together with Thrusters) the most important module for AX. Engineer it with Charge Enhanced + Super Conduits Experimental. This can also be done in `WYRD by The Dweller`.
3. Engineer your Heatsinks with Ammo capacity. This can be done in `MEENE by Ram Tah`.
4. Engineer your Power Plant to supply your upgraded modules with enough power. Armored Engineering will do the trick. `Falicity Farseer in DECIAT` offers G1, `Marco Qwent in SIRIUS` G4. Both are accessible very early.
5. A Frameshift Drive with Longer Range adds a lot of quality-of-life. `Falicity Farseer in DECIAT`.

### Guardian Modules
Note Guardian Modules have significant power requirements.
> See Guardian Unlock Guides: [Guardian Unlocks](/en/guardianunlocks)
Short Video by CMDR EXIGEUOUS: [Guardian Gauss Cannon](https://youtu.be/RcWVQf0NUag?is=G2Wl1p2aETixJdqL).
{.is-info}

1. Remove both 3C Enhanced AX Multicannons.
	Replace them with 2B fixed Guardian Gauss Cannons.
2. Optionally, replace Module Reinforcement Packages with Guardian variants.
	This is completely optional, the benefits are not game-changing.


## Final Upgrades
Further engineering to match the [Meta Chief](<https://edsy.org/#/L=J900000H4C0S40,LYS10LYS10L3I10LXC10LXC10EhtH07P_W0,CjwG-2G002P000S_00CjwG-2G002P000S_00CjwG-2G002P000S_00CjwG-2G002P000S_00,9onG05I_W0AAAG03K_W0APoG05I_W0AdtG05J_W0AtyJ03G_W0B8gG03L_W0BLeG05G_W0Bb600,15OG05I_W015OG05I_W015OG05I_W0,25T006kK20072000Iw2020n001-D00,Meta_0AX_0Chieftan,AXIN>), specialised for Interceptor combat in Non-Human Signal Sources.

You have the choice of how many materials you want to invest. The next recommended Modules to engineer are:
1. Armor
2. Hull Reinforcement Packages
3. Then more optional improvements, such as Sensors or Lifesupport.
4. Guardian Module Reinforcements remain optional.
